🎵 Freaky Sheets

Sheet music that scrolls for you

Upload your music and play along while the page scrolls automatically. You can even let it listen to your playing and follow you.

Supported files: MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) and MIDI (.mid, .midi). You can also drag & drop a file anywhere on this page.
